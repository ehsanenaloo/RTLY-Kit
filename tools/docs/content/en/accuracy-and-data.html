Our principle

RTLY-Kit has no runtime dependency, so its reference data is embedded in the package. Most of it has no official machine-readable publication, so it was compiled from public third-party sources. This page lists them and says plainly what is verified, what is only consistent with other sources, and what is not checked at all. Where a claim is weaker than it sounds, we say so.

Note. Source lookups were done on 2026-10-07 and 2026-10-08. Banks merge and rename, operators reassign ranges and moon sightings differ, so any table can drift. The data is best-effort and is not an official registry; do not use it alone for legal or financial decisions.

The two-source rule

A bank card BIN, a Sheba bank code or a national-code prefix is included only when at least two independent sources agree. An entry found in one source, or where sources conflict, is left out on purpose. Consequently a missing entry means "unknown", never "invalid": a valid card can come back with getBankName() equal to null. Some community datasets share ancestry, so agreement is strong evidence but not fully independent confirmation. Only facts (numeric ranges and the names they map to) were taken; no source text or code was copied.

Prayer times: status per method

The calculation is clean-room, from standard equations. The only meaningful external check is to compute a city and date and compare with the authority's own published timetable, within the minute rounding of both sides (the tests allow plus or minus 2 minutes). Aggregator websites were not counted, because they use the same published angles and agreement would be circular.

Sunrise and sunset were also spot-checked against the NOAA Solar Calculator (12 city and date pairs, all within one minute). Not tested: Asr under the Hanafi convention, high latitudes, elevation, and any date outside those listed. Different authorities use different angles; check that the method matches the one your users follow. The Makkah Ramadan decision follows the civil date, not the sunset-based Islamic day.

Known limitations. (1) The Umm al-Qura table is not verified month by month against KACST. (2) Iranian Islamic holidays can differ from the announcement by 1-2 days. (3) Bank, Sheba, operator and national-code tables are incomplete and derived from community datasets. (4) The Karachi, MWL and ISNA angles and the Tehran method's Isha and Asr are not verified against an official timetable. (5) Arabic number words are limited as described above.

Found a mistake? Please open an issue on GitHub with a source. A better source is the quickest way to get a table corrected.
