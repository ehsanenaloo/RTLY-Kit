The limits below are deliberate. They keep every call cheap and predictable, and keep untrusted input from turning into a wrapped-around date or an expensive computation. Exceeding one always raises a documented RtlyKitException (see Error handling) or, for validators, an invalid Result.

Calendar year ranges

Every supported date of every calendar maps to a Gregorian year between 1 and 9999, so any value the classes produce can be held by a DateTimeImmutable without wraparound. The ranges are the public constants MIN_YEAR and MAX_YEAR:

CalendarMIN_YEARMAX_YEARGregorian date of 1/1 of those years
Jalali-62093770001-03-21 .. 9998-03-20
Hijri196650622-07-19 .. 9998-10-12
Hebrew3762137590001-09-06 .. 9998-10-15
 
<?php
require __DIR__.'/vendor/autoload.php';

use RtlyKit\Calendar\{Hebrew, Hijri, Jalali};

foreach ([Jalali::class, Hijri::class, Hebrew::class] as $class) {
    printf(
        "%-7s %5d..%-5d  %s .. %s\n",
        (new ReflectionClass($class))->getShortName(),
        $class::MIN_YEAR,
        $class::MAX_YEAR,
        $class::create($class::MIN_YEAR, 1, 1)->toGregorian()->format('Y-m-d'),
        $class::create($class::MAX_YEAR, 1, 1)->toGregorian()->format('Y-m-d'),
    );
}
// Jalali   -620..9377   0001-03-21 .. 9998-03-20
// Hijri       1..9665   0622-07-19 .. 9998-10-12
// Hebrew   3762..13759  0001-09-06 .. 9998-10-15

Every entry point obeys the range: make, create, createFromFormat, Unix timestamps, add*() / sub*() (including huge deltas), the helpers and the Carbon macros. A value outside the range throws InvalidDateException; nothing returns a wrapped-around date, and no TypeError or ValueError escapes.

Deltas and timestamps

  • An addDays() / subDays() (and hours, minutes, seconds) amount larger than the span of the whole supported calendar (about 3.7 million days, or the same span in hours, minutes or seconds) is rejected up front with InvalidDateException; a smaller amount that lands outside the supported years is rejected as well.
  • A Unix timestamp must fall within Gregorian years 1 to 9999 (with a little slack for the time zone); anything further out is rejected.
  • Hebrew::addMonths() and addYears() run in constant time, however large the amount.

String-year thresholds

A string such as 1405/01/01 or 1405-01-01 passed to make() or a helper is read as the calendar's own date when the year is below the threshold, and as a Gregorian string otherwise:

ClassOwn-calendar yearOtherwise
Jalali / jdate()below 1700Gregorian
Hijri / hdate()below 1700Gregorian
Hebrew / hebrew_date()3000 or moreGregorian

To pass a real Gregorian date below the threshold (for example the year 999), use a DateTimeImmutable, which is never reinterpreted. See the FAQ.

Umm al-Qura range

  • The embedded Umm al-Qura month table covers AH 1300 to 1500 (1882-11-12 to 2077-11-16). Outside it, and always for HijriVariant::Tabular, Hijri uses arithmetic rules that can differ from observation by a day or two. Hijri::hasUmmAlQuraData($year) tells whether a year is covered.
  • IranHolidays reports Islamic holidays only inside that range. Outside it, only the fixed Jalali holidays are returned.
